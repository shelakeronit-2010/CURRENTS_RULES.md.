Thank you for checking before launch. DailyDrop's workflow, as you describe it, is permitted on the Free plan. A paid plan is needed only for more volume, and AI summaries need separate terms (point 5).

1. Commercial use

Commercial use is allowed on our self-service plans, including Free, for apps that show short previews and link out to the original article.

2. Display to app users

You may show the title, the description preview, the publisher name, the publication date and the original article URL, with the attribution in point 6.

3. Temporary caching

A backend cache of up to 24 hours, for performance and de-duplication, is short-lived operational caching. Our terms permit it. Please delete cached items after 24 hours and do not build an archive or database from them.

4. Advertising

Yes. You may show advertisements in the app alongside this metadata.

5. AI summaries

AI summaries shown to your users are not included on the Free plan or our other self-service plans. Our pricing page lists them as "Not included by default | Scope with us before launch". If you want to add them later, contact us with the details, and we will quote separate terms.

6. Attribution

- Currents: show "Powered by Currents News API" with a link to https://currentsapi.services/, near the news feed or on the news screens of the app.
- Publishers: show the publisher name and link directly to the original article on every item. This meets what Currents requires. Our attribution clause does not replace any obligation you owe to a publisher under its own terms.

7. Images

Currents returns links to images hosted by publishers and does not grant any image licence. Your plan to use images from separately licensed sources is consistent with our terms.

8. Lowest-cost plan

The Free plan covers DailyDrop while you build and launch: 250 requests per day and up to 20 results per request. Your request count depends on how often your backend refreshes, not on how many people use the app, because your 24-hour cache serves your users.

For example (not your figures), refreshing 8 news categories every 30 minutes uses 384 requests a day, which is above Free. The Builder plan (USD 69 per month) covers that with room to spare: 2,500 requests per day, 50 results per request so each refresh fills a fuller feed, and 6 months of history for search. Plan details: https://currentsapi.services/en/pricing
